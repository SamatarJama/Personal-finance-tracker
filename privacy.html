Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Personal Finance Tracker is a non-commercial budgeting application developed for the developer's personal use only.

Information processed

With the account holder's consent, the application may access bank account balances and transaction information through Enable Banking.

Purpose

Banking information is used solely for personal budgeting, expense categorization and financial analysis.

Storage and sharing

The application is intended to store financial information in a private environment. Data is not sold or shared for advertising purposes. Enable Banking and participating banks process information as necessary to provide account connectivity.

Access and withdrawal

The account holder can revoke banking access through the relevant bank or provider. Stored information can be deleted by the application owner.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the developer using the data protection email registered with Enable Banking.
